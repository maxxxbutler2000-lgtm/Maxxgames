Hextris

Rotate the hexagon and clear matching colors.

Hextris is a speed puzzle where blocks fall toward a hexagon. Stack matching colors and clear them before the board fills up.

Back to downloads

Basics

Controls

  • Left / A: rotate the hexagon left.
  • Right / D: rotate the hexagon right.
  • Touchscreen: tap the left or right side of the screen to rotate.

Score

Strategy

  1. Keep stacks low and avoid letting one side get too tall.
  2. Rotate early so falling blocks land on matching colors.
  3. Look at the next incoming colors instead of only the block that is about to land.
  4. When the speed increases, make safe clears instead of waiting for perfect combos.
