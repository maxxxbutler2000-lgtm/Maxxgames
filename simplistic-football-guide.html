Simplistic Football Guide

Goal

Score more goals than the other team before the timer ends.

Controls

  • WASD moves your selected player.
  • Shift sprints while moving.
  • J passes to a teammate.
  • K sends a through ball into space.
  • L steals or tackles.
  • Space charges and releases a shot.
  • Q switches your selected player.
  • R restarts kickoff.
  • C changes the camera.

How To Play

Use passing to move the ball up the field, sprint when there is open space, and shoot when you are close enough to the goal. If the other team has the ball, switch to the closest player and tackle.

Best Tips

  • Pass before defenders crowd you.
  • Use through balls when a teammate is running ahead.
  • Hold Space longer for a stronger shot.
  • Try different match lengths and difficulty settings.
