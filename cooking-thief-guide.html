Cooking Thief

Steal, survive, and somehow make soup.

Cooking Thief is a short action game where you play a thief who gets forced into cooking.

Play Cooking Thief

Basics

How to play

  1. Move with the keyboard. Dodge enemies and explore the room.
  2. Aim with the mouse. Use your weapon when you need space.
  3. Find new weapons. If one stops working, look for another option.
  4. Collect soup items. The goal is not just fighting; you need to cook too.
  5. Keep moving. Standing still makes it easier to get trapped.

Action

Tips

Clear a path

Do not fight everything forever. Make enough room to reach ingredients.

Swap weapons

The itch page tip says to find a new weapon if you cannot use the current one.

Watch corners

Top-down action games punish corner traps, so keep an exit route open.
