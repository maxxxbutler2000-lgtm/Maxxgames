DMUX

Smash cars in low-poly derby arenas.

DMUX is a free/libre 3D derby-style shooter with low-poly vehicle combat.

Download DMUX

Basics

How to play

  1. Pick a vehicle. Choose a car or preset loadout.
  2. Drive the arena. Drift, dodge, and line up attacks.
  3. Shoot rivals. Use weapons while moving.
  4. Use collisions. Derby combat rewards hard hits.
  5. Try local or online play. Builds may change as the prototype grows.

Derby

Tips

Keep speed

Slow cars are easy targets.

Turn wide

Wide turns help set up rams and shots.

Use cover

Arena objects can break enemy aim.
