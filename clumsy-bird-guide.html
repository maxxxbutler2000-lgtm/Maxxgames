Clumsy Bird

Flap through the gaps and keep flying.

Clumsy Bird is a quick arcade game. Your goal is simple: stay in the air and pass through as many obstacles as possible.

Back to downloads

Basics

Controls

  • Space, click, or tap: flap upward.
  • Do nothing to fall downward.
  • Restart after a crash and try for a higher score.

Timing

Strategy

  1. Use small taps instead of big panic taps.
  2. Watch the next gap, not just the obstacle right in front of you.
  3. Stay near the middle of the screen so you can correct up or down.
  4. Do not flap at the top of a gap unless you need to avoid falling fast.
