Hypersomnia

Fast top-down multiplayer shooting.

Hypersomnia is a free open-source competitive shooter that runs in the browser.

Play Hypersomnia

Basics

How to play

  1. Move fast. Do not stand still in open space.
  2. Aim carefully. Track enemies before firing.
  3. Use cover. Walls and corners help you survive.
  4. Learn weapons. Each one has different timing.
  5. Play the tutorial. It teaches the controls quickly.

Combat

Tips

Pre-aim corners

Expect enemies around doors and hallways.

Reload safely

Reload behind cover, not while crossing open space.

Keep distance

Back up when enemies rush you.
