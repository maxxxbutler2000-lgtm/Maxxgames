CSS 3D Dungeon Guide

Goal

Explore the dungeon, read clues, use items, and solve the room puzzle.

How To Play

  • Look for scrolls and items you can click.
  • Read clues before pressing random things.
  • Try items when the dungeon gives you a hint.
  • Use the on-screen controls if they appear.

Best Tip

Treat it like an escape room. Click carefully, remember clues, and check corners for hidden details.
