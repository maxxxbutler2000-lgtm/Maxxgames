Astray Guide

Goal

Guide the ball through each dark 3D maze without falling off the path.

How To Play

  • Use the keyboard controls shown in the game to move.
  • Move slowly near corners and narrow paths.
  • Watch the shadows and walls to judge where the ball can go.
  • Reach the end of the level to move on.

Best Tip

Do not rush. Astray is easier when you tap controls lightly instead of holding them down forever.
