Amazing Track

Tap once to turn and stay on the track.

Amazing Track is a minimalist endless runner where one button changes your direction. Survive as long as possible.

Play Amazing Track

Basics

How to play

  1. Tap or click. Each tap changes the direction.
  2. Stay on the path. Falling off ends the run.
  3. Watch ahead. The track turns quickly.
  4. Chase high scores. Runs are short, so retry fast.
  5. Use touch or mouse. It works with simple one-button input.

Runner

Tips

Tap early

React before the edge, not while you are already falling.

Find rhythm

Short turns feel easier when you tap in a steady rhythm.

Reset fast

Short games are about quick retries and improving a little each time.
