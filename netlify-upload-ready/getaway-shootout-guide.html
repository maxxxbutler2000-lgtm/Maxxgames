Getaway Shootout Guide

Goal

Reach the getaway exit before everyone else. Every round is a messy race where players hop, shove, shoot, and grab weapons on the way.

Controls

The game is built around a tiny control set. Each player mainly uses left, right, and action. The action button picks up items, shoots, or uses what you are holding.

How To Win

  • Keep moving toward the exit even when you fall over.
  • Grab weapons only when they are already on your path.
  • Shoot players who are ahead of you, not players who are already behind.
  • Use platforms and walls to bounce yourself forward.
  • Watch the map, because the exit can be above, below, or far away.

Weapons

Weapons appear around the level. Run over one and use the action button. Some are better for close range, while others are useful when another player is far ahead.

Best Tip

Do not stop moving. The controls are silly on purpose, so the player who keeps hopping forward usually beats the player who waits for a perfect shot.
