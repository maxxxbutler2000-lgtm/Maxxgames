HexGL

Race fast, stay clean, and master the track.

HexGL is a futuristic 3D WebGL racing game. Your goal is to keep speed through the course without smashing into the walls.

Back to downloads

Driving

Controls

  • Arrow keys: steer and control the racer.
  • Keyboard mode: pick keyboard controls on the start screen.
  • Quality setting: lower quality if a Chromebook struggles.
  • Offline download: unzip the folder and open index.html in Chrome.

Racing

Strategy

  1. Use tiny steering corrections instead of sharp turns.
  2. Learn where the corners are before trying for a perfect time.
  3. Stay away from walls because crashes cost more time than slowing slightly.
  4. If performance feels rough, restart with a lower quality setting.
