XQuest

Blast through space enemies and collect scrap.

XQuest is a free open-source HTML5 space shooter with arcade and puzzle modes.

Play XQuest

Basics

How to play

  1. Move constantly. Dodge enemy shots and hazards.
  2. Shoot enemies. Clear the screen before you get boxed in.
  3. Collect scrap. Rewards help your score.
  4. Try both modes. Arcade is faster, puzzle mode is more planned.
  5. Restart fast. Learn enemy patterns each run.

Space

Tips

Use space

Stay away from corners when enemies are closing in.

Prioritize threats

Destroy enemies that are closest or shooting the most.

Watch patterns

Most arcade shooters get easier once you know the movement rhythm.
