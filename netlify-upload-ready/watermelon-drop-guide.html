Watermelon Drop Guide

Goal

Drop fruit into the box and merge matching fruit. Keep combining fruit until you make the biggest one.

How To Play

  • Move the drop position left or right.
  • Drop fruit carefully so matching fruit touch.
  • Two matching fruit merge into a bigger fruit.
  • Do not let the fruit pile reach the top.

Best Tips

  • Keep big fruit on one side so the board stays organized.
  • Do not block small fruit under huge fruit.
  • Think one drop ahead and watch the Next fruit.
