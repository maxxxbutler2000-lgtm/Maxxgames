Destination Sol

Explore space, fight enemies, and upgrade your ship.

Destination Sol is an open-source arcade/RPG where you fly through a star system, mine, fight, and improve your ship.

Download Destination Sol

Basics

How to play

  1. Fly your ship. Move carefully around planets and enemies.
  2. Fight pirates. Use weapons while dodging shots.
  3. Mine asteroids. Gather resources for money.
  4. Upgrade often. Better ships and gear make space safer.
  5. Explore planets. Land and look for useful shops.

Space

Tips

Do not rush

Early ships are weak, so pick fights carefully.

Upgrade weapons

More damage makes every trip safer.

Keep moving

A moving ship is harder to hit.
