Retro Bowl Guide

Goal

Build a strong football team, call good plays, and score more touchdowns than the other team.

How To Play

  • On offense, choose passes and runs that match what the defense gives you.
  • Throw to open receivers instead of forcing risky passes.
  • Use short gains when a deep pass is covered.
  • Between games, upgrade players and manage the team.

Best Tips

  • Protect the ball. Interceptions hurt more than short throws.
  • Upgrade key offensive players first.
  • Use the sideline when you need to stop the clock.
