A Dark Room

Light the fire and grow from nothing.

A Dark Room is a slow-burn survival adventure. It begins tiny, then gradually reveals crafting, workers, exploration, and danger.

Back to downloads

Start

Early game

  1. Light the fire whenever it gets low.
  2. Collect wood and build simple structures as they unlock.
  3. Assign workers when the village begins to grow.
  4. Keep enough resources for important upgrades before spending everything.
  5. Offline download: unzip the folder and open index.html.

Survival

Strategy

  • Read new text carefully because it hints at new mechanics.
  • Balance gathering, building, and exploration instead of rushing one path.
  • Stockpile supplies before heading into the world.
  • Expect the game to expand slowly; patience is part of the design.
