How to survive, craft, hunt, and build your camp.

This guide covers the core controls, your first day, crafting recipes, hunting animals, fishing, weather, exploration, and tool progression.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse

  • WASD: move
  • Mouse: look around
  • Shift: sprint
  • Space: jump
  • E: interact
  • R: use equipped tool or place a structure
  • Q: unequip current item
  • F: eat food or use healing items
  • C: crouch
  • V: open inventory panel
  • I: open equipment menu
  • X: open crafting
  • 1-9, 0, -, =, [, ], \: quick equip items

Controller

  • Left stick: move
  • Right stick: look around
  • L3: sprint
  • L2: interact
  • R2: use equipped item
  • Square: unequip
  • Triangle: eat
  • R1 / L1: next or previous item slot
  • Share: open inventory
  • Touchpad: open crafting
  • Menu: pause

First day

What to do first

  1. Gather branches and river rocks. Branches are on the ground, and river rocks are usually near water.
  2. Craft a campfire kit. You need 4 branches and 3 river rocks. It gives warmth and lets you cook food.
  3. Craft a stone axe. Use 2 river rocks and 1 branch, then equip it from the equipment menu or quick slot.
  4. Chop trees for wood. Equip the axe and press R on keyboard, or R2 on controller.
  5. Eat early food. Berries, mushrooms, herbs, and fish help keep hunger from reaching zero.
  6. Build shelter. Storms hurt you if you are not protected.

Hunting Mobs

How to hunt

  1. Build a crafting bench and reach the recipes for a bow.
  2. Craft a Bow from 2 rope and 3 branches.
  3. Craft Arrow Bundles from feathers and branches.
  4. Equip the bow from the equipment menu or quick slot.
  5. Hold right-click to draw, aim at the animal, then release to fire.

Animals

  • Rabbit: found in forests. Drops raw meat and hide.
  • Bird: found in forests and hills. Drops feathers.
  • Desert lizards and tortoises: decorative, so do not waste arrows on them.

Water food

Fishing

  1. Craft a fishing rod with 3 branches and 1 rope.
  2. Find a fishing spot near water.
  3. Press E on keyboard or L2 on controller to cast.
  4. Wait for a bite.
  5. Press interact again quickly to reel in the fish.
  6. Cook fish at your fire pit for more hunger restore than eating it raw.

Camp upgrades

Progression

Level 1: Survival Camp

Build the basics: fire pit, shelter, crafting bench, drying rack, and fishing rod.

Level 2: Functional Camp

Unlock bow, arrows, storage, garden plots, snare traps, canvas tent, machete, and grappling hook.

Level 3: Wilderness Basecamp

Build advanced structures like a log cabin, smithing station, smoker, and weather vane. Unlock metal tools, diamond tools, lantern, compass, and hang glider.

Stay alive

Survival Tips

  • Cook food whenever you can because cooked food restores more hunger.
  • Stockpile rope, branches, rocks, and wood near your crafting bench.
  • Bring a torch or lantern into caves because caves are dark and dangerous.
  • Do not explore too far during storms, cold snaps, or heat waves.
  • Use storage boxes to separate food, building materials, and rare resources.
  • Search rocky regions, hills, mountains, and caves for ore and crystals.
  • Save from the config menu with Tab on keyboard or L3 + R3 on controller.
