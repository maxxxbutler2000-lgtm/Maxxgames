Maxx Chatroom

Open the chatroom and talk with friends.

Maxx Chatroom is a live chat site made by Maxx. Use a nickname, keep it friendly, and do not post private info.

Open Chatroom

Basics

How to use it

  1. Press Open Chatroom. The chat opens in a new tab.
  2. Use a nickname. Pick a fun name that does not reveal who you are.
  3. Type a message. Keep messages short and friendly.
  4. Refresh if needed. If the chat gets stuck, reload the page.
  5. Come back to MaxxGames. Use the browser tab to return to the games list.

Important

Chat safety

No private info

Do not share your full name, address, school, phone number, passwords, or private accounts.

Be normal and kind

No bullying, threats, spam, or trying to trick people.

Leave weird chats

If someone makes the chat uncomfortable, close it and tell a trusted adult.
