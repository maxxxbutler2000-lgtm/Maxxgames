Lix

Guide tiny characters through puzzle levels.

Lix is a free open-source action-puzzle game inspired by Lemmings.

Download Lix

Basics

How to play

  1. Watch the lix walk. They keep moving unless you change the level.
  2. Assign skills. Use builders, bashers, blockers, and other tools.
  3. Save enough lix. Each level has a goal number.
  4. Plan first. Random clicking can trap your team.
  5. Retry puzzles. Finding the solution is the point.

Tips

Pause and think

Work out the route before spending skills.

Protect the crowd

One mistake can send everyone into danger.

Use builders wisely

Bridges are powerful but easy to waste.
