OpenClonk

Mine, build, fight, and solve sandbox missions.

OpenClonk is a free open-source action sandbox with single-player and multiplayer modes.

Download OpenClonk

Basics

How to play

  1. Control a clonk. Move your character through destructible worlds.
  2. Use tools. Dig, build, throw, fight, and carry items.
  3. Complete missions. Some levels are about settling, others are combat or puzzles.
  4. Try multiplayer. OpenClonk supports co-op and versus play.
  5. Download first. This one is a desktop game, not a browser game.

Sandbox

Tips

Use the terrain

Digging and building can solve problems better than fighting.

Learn tools

Each item has a job, so test them in easy missions first.

Play co-op

Many objectives are more fun with another player helping.
