LittleJS Breakout

Bounce the ball and smash every brick.

A simple polished Breakout arcade game made with the open-source LittleJS engine.

Play Breakout

Basics

How to play

  1. Move the paddle. Keep the ball from falling.
  2. Break bricks. Hit every brick to clear the board.
  3. Aim with the paddle. Where the ball hits changes its angle.
  4. Stay calm. The ball gets harder to follow when it speeds up.
  5. Protect the bottom. Missing the ball costs the run.

Arcade

Tips

Use edges

Hit the ball with the paddle edge to send sharper angles.

Clear sides

Side gaps can trap the ball above bricks for big clears.

Watch the ball

Ignore distractions and track the ball first.
