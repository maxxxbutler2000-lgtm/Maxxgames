The Last Stand

Defend Yggdrasil with a ballista, traps, and timing.

Monsters attack from both sides. Shoot with the ballista, place traps, and survive every wave.

Play or Download

Basics

How to play

  1. Aim with the mouse. Move the cursor to point the ballista.
  2. Click to shoot. Hold the click to charge a stronger shot.
  3. Reload with R. Keep the ballista ready before big groups arrive.
  4. Buy traps. Use earned money to slow and damage monsters.
  5. Survive the waves. Protect the middle tower until the level is clear.

Defense

Strategy

Charge shots

A charged ballista shot is better for bigger monsters and packed groups.

Trap both sides

Do not spend everything on one side because enemies can attack from either direction.

Reload early

Reload during quiet moments so you are not empty when enemies reach the tower.
