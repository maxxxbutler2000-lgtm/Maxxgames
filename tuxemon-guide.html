Tuxemon

Catch creatures and battle through an open-source RPG.

Tuxemon is a free monster-catching RPG with open-source code and free assets.

Download Tuxemon

Basics

How to play

  1. Explore towns and routes. Talk to people and find new areas.
  2. Collect Tuxemon. Build a team of creatures.
  3. Battle trainers. Use strong matchups and items.
  4. Level up. Stronger creatures survive harder fights.
  5. Save often. RPG progress matters.

Tips
