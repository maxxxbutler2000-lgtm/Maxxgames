Warzone 2100

Build a base, research tech, and command armies.

Warzone 2100 is a free open-source 3D RTS with campaign, skirmish, and multiplayer.

Download Warzone 2100

Basics

How to play

  1. Build power. Power keeps your base producing.
  2. Research tech. Unlock stronger weapons and bodies.
  3. Design units. Mix weapons, bodies, and propulsion.
  4. Defend your base. Towers and units stop raids.
  5. Attack when ready. Scout before sending everything.

RTS

Tips

Research nonstop

Falling behind in tech makes every fight harder.

Use groups

Send units together instead of one at a time.

Repair often

Pull damaged units back before losing them.
