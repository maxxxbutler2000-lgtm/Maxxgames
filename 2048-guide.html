2048

Slide, merge, and build the 2048 tile.

2048 is a compact puzzle game where matching numbers combine. Keep your board organized and aim for one high-value corner.

Back to downloads

Basics

How to play

  1. Use arrow keys or swipes. Every move slides all tiles in one direction.
  2. Merge matching numbers. Two 2 tiles become 4, two 4 tiles become 8, and so on.
  3. Watch new tiles. A new 2 or 4 appears after every move.
  4. Reach 2048. Make the 2048 tile to win, then keep playing for a higher score.
  5. Avoid filling the board. If there are no moves left, the game ends.

Plan

Strategy

Pick a corner

Keep your biggest tile in one corner and build smaller tiles toward it.

Use three directions

Try not to move away from your chosen corner unless there is no other useful move.

Keep a chain

Arrange values like 128, 64, 32, and 16 near each other so merges stay controlled.
