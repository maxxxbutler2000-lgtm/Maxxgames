Flappy Race

Race like Flappy Bird with Mario Kart-style items.

Flappy Race is an open-source Godot racing game with bots, items, single-player, and online multiplayer.

Play Flappy Race

Basics

How to play

  1. Tap or press to flap. Stay in the air and avoid obstacles.
  2. Race to the finish. It is not only survival; you are trying to beat other racers.
  3. Use items. Grab item boxes and use power-ups to gain an advantage.
  4. Try bots first. Practice single-player before online races.
  5. Use multiplayer when ready. The game supports public servers and self-hosting on desktop.

Racing

Tips

Small taps win

Short controlled taps are better than panicking and bouncing into obstacles.

Save items

Use power-ups when they can actually pass someone or save you from a crash.

Practice lines

Learn each track's gaps so you spend less time reacting late.
