Teeworlds

Jump, swing, and shoot in fast multiplayer matches.

Teeworlds is a free open-source online 2D multiplayer shooter for major operating systems.

Download Teeworlds

Basics

How to play

  1. Move fast. Jump and change direction often.
  2. Use the hook. Swing around maps and pull yourself to platforms.
  3. Grab weapons. Better weapons help you win fights.
  4. Watch health. Pick up hearts and armor when low.
  5. Play online. Most of the fun is in multiplayer servers.
