Style Studio

Make outfits that match each style challenge.

Style Studio is a quick makeover game where you choose hair, makeup, clothing, and accessories. Finish the look to score points.

Play Style Studio

Basics

How to play

  1. Read the challenge. It tells you what kind of look to make.
  2. Pick hair and makeup. Try colors that fit the vibe.
  3. Choose an outfit. Beach, party, cozy, and cute all score differently.
  4. Add an accessory. The right accessory can complete the challenge.
  5. Press Finish Look. You get more points when your choices match the prompt.

Plan

Easy tips

Match the theme

Beach looks like bright colors. Party looks like bold colors. Cozy looks softer.

Use the challenge button

Press New Challenge when you want a fresh style goal.

Keep scoring

The score keeps adding up, so you can keep playing and beat your best number.
