OpenMinesweeper

Find every mine without clicking one.

Minesweeper is a logic puzzle. Numbers tell you how many mines touch that square, and your job is to reveal all safe squares.

Basics

How to play

  1. Reveal squares. Click covered tiles to uncover them.
  2. Read the numbers. A number means that many mines are touching the square.
  3. Mark suspected mines. Use flags on tiles you believe contain mines.
  4. Clear safe spaces. Use the numbers to figure out which hidden tiles are safe.
  5. Win the board. Reveal every safe tile without opening a mine.

Logic

Useful patterns

Exact count

If a number touches the same amount of covered tiles as its value, all those covered tiles are mines.

Already flagged

If a number already has enough flagged mines touching it, every other covered tile touching that number is safe.

Slow down

Before guessing, scan nearby numbers. Minesweeper usually gives you a logical move somewhere else on the board.
