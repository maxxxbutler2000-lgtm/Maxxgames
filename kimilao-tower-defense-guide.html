3D Tower Defense

Use 3D towers to block and destroy enemies.

This Unity tower defense game lets enemies move around the map, so tower placement and blocking matter more than in a fixed-path game.

Download Game

Basics

How to play

  1. Move the camera. Use WASD to look around the battlefield.
  2. Zoom in and out. Scroll the mouse wheel to inspect enemies and towers.
  3. Build turrets. Left click to select or place turrets.
  4. Upgrade turrets. Right click a turret when you want to improve it.
  5. Block smartly. Enemies can move freely, so force them into paths your towers can cover.

Defense

Strategy

Make choke points

Use towers to narrow enemy movement so more shots hit the same group.

Upgrade busy towers

Upgrade towers that already cover the most enemy traffic.

Watch the whole map

Since enemies are not locked to one path, keep checking for new weak spots.
